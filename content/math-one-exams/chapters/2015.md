### 第 4 题

B. $\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{3}} d\theta \int_{\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\sin2\theta}}^{\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\sin2\theta}} f(r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta) r \, \mathrm{d}r$.

D. $\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{3}} d\theta \int_{\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\sin2\theta}}^{\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\sin2\theta}} f(r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta) r \, \mathrm{d}r$.

### 第 5 题

### 第 6 题
